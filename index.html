Doodle Planet Uploader

A private tool the Doodle Planet channel owner uses to upload their own educational videos.

What it does

  1. The channel owner signs in once with their Google Account (OAuth 2.0).
  2. The tool uploads a finished video to the owner’s own channel as private, with its captions and thumbnail. AI use is disclosed in the video’s description, and YouTube’s altered-content label is applied where YouTube’s policy requires it.
  3. The owner reviews the video in YouTube Studio and publishes it manually.

Who it is for

Only the owner of the Doodle Planet channel. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else’s data.
